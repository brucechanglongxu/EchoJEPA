\begin{abstract}
Standard interpretability tools for medical foundation models report
\emph{whether} a property is decodable or \emph{where} pixels matter, but
say little about the internal \emph{state} the model tracks across time.
For periodic biological systems such as the heart, the dynamical axis is
the most diagnostic. We introduce \sonostate, a post-hoc \emph{dynamical
probe} for an echocardiographic V-JEPA-2 ViT-L encoder: a unit-norm
state head $S_\psi$ and a residual transition $f_\theta$ initialized to
the identity, trained with the encoder fully frozen. Identity init is the
key methodological commitment: any non-trivial structure that emerges is
information already encoded by the foundation model, not capacity added by
the probe. A 21-cell ablation on EchoNet-Dynamic isolates a single
on-sphere uniformity term as load-bearing: with it, the latent space
collapses onto an intrinsically $3$-dimensional closed manifold (TwoNN
$\widehat{d}_\mathrm{ID}\!\approx\!3$, mean cosine loop closure $0.75$);
without it, intrinsic dimension inflates to $\sim\!6$, loop closure drops
to $0.55$, and a downstream LVEF probe collapses from $R^2{=}0.48$ to
$0.14$. We propose this three-metric geometry probe (intrinsic dimension,
loop closure, cycle-consistent round-trip error) as a cheap, label-free
evaluation recipe for medical video FMs: it identifies degenerate
representations that an output-level evaluation would miss.

\keywords{mechanistic interpretability \and medical foundation models
          \and JEPA \and echocardiography \and dynamical probing}
\end{abstract}

% ===== 1. Introduction ==================================================
\section{Introduction}

Foundation models (FMs) increasingly underpin medical imaging
pipelines~\cite{vjepa2,echonet,echoclip}, but high-stakes deployment needs
more than saliency maps and downstream metrics. Two echocardiograms can
yield the same predicted ejection fraction by very different internal
computations, only one of which generalizes. We need probes of the
\emph{internal state} the model tracks, not just its output.

Mechanistic interpretability~\cite{olah2020circuits,elhage2022solu}
asks a sharper question: can we identify the features and computations a
network uses, in a form that matches our prior scientific knowledge of the
domain? Medical imaging is unusually well-suited because we have a strong
domain prior --- the heart is a (nearly) periodic dynamical system whose
state is well-defined and whose evolution is governed by physiology.

We instantiate this idea with \sonostate, a \emph{dynamical probe} for an
echocardiographic V-JEPA-2 encoder. Standard linear
probing~\cite{alain2017probing} measures static decodability; we instead
ask whether the encoder's trajectory through time admits a low-dimensional
predictable structure. Because our transition operator is initialized to
the identity, any structure it surfaces is information already in the
frozen encoder, not capacity we have added.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(i) An identity-initialized dynamical probe for medical video FMs that is
faithful in the mechanistic-interpretability sense: at convergence it
reveals representation properties of the frozen backbone, not those of an
auxiliary head. (ii) A 21-cell ablation that isolates a single sphere
uniformity term as the only load-bearing ingredient for a clean
$3$-dimensional cardiac-cycle manifold. (iii) A three-metric geometry
probe (intrinsic dimension, loop closure, cycle-consistent round-trip
error) as a label-free evaluation recipe that flags collapsed
representations a downstream LVEF probe would miss.

% ===== 2. Probe ========================================================
\section{The \sonostate Probe}

\paragraph{Architecture.}
Given a $16$-frame, $8$\,fps clip $x_t$, the frozen encoder $E_\phi$
produces $N{\times}D$ spatio-temporal tokens. Two trainable modules sit
on top: a \emph{state head} $S_\psi$ that mean-pools tokens, applies
LayerNorm and a linear projection to $\mathbb{R}^d$, and L2-normalizes,
yielding $\mathbf{z}_t \in \mathcal{S}^{d-1}$; and a residual
\emph{transition}
$f_\theta(\mathbf{z}) = \mathrm{normalize}(\mathbf{z} + \mathrm{e}^\alpha\,\mathrm{MLP}(\mathbf{z}))$
with hidden width $512$. The last layer of MLP is zero-initialized and
$\alpha\!=\!-4.6$ so that $f_\theta(\mathbf{z}) = \mathbf{z}$ at $t{=}0$
of probe training. \sonostate cannot add temporal structure that is not
already implicit in $E_\phi$; any deviation from persistence at
convergence is therefore evidence about the encoder.

\paragraph{Training objective.}
$S_\psi$ and $f_\theta$ are trained on consecutive clip pairs from
EchoNet-Dynamic with stop-gradient on the target encoder:
\[
\mathcal{L}
= \underbrace{\bigl\lVert f_\theta(\mathbf{z}_t) - \mathbf{z}^\star_{t+1}\bigr\rVert_1
  + \bigl(1 - \cos(f_\theta(\mathbf{z}_t), \mathbf{z}^\star_{t+1})\bigr)}_{\mathcal{L}_\mathrm{forecast}}
+ \lambda_\mathrm{u}\,\mathcal{L}_\mathrm{uniform},
\]
where $\mathcal{L}_\mathrm{uniform}$ is the Wang--Isola sphere uniformity
term~\cite{wang2020uniformity}. We sweep
$d \in \{32,64,128,256,512\}$, $\lambda_\mathrm{u} \in \{0,10^{-2},10^{-1},5\}$,
forecast loss $\in \{L_1,\cos,L_1{+}\cos\}$, encoder
$\in \{\text{frozen},\text{ftuned}\}$, transition init
$\in \{\text{identity},\text{noresidual},\text{random}\}$, and a
shuffled-time control --- $21$ cells in total.

% ===== 3. Findings ======================================================
\section{What the Probe Reveals about the Encoder}

\paragraph{Finding 1: a $3$-D cardiac-cycle manifold.}
We extract overlapping clips from $751$ held-out videos, pass each through
$E_\phi \!\to\! S_\psi$, and project pooled $\mathbf{z}_t$ to $2$-D with
PCA. Closed loops emerge \emph{within} videos, with consistent orientation
and substantial cross-patient overlap, despite the encoder having received
no ECG or phase supervision. Quantitatively, the TwoNN intrinsic
dimension~\cite{facco2017twonn} of the pooled state cloud is
$\widehat{d}_\mathrm{ID} = 3.03,\,2.89,\,2.97,\,3.03,\,3.10$ at ambient
$d{=}32,64,128,256,512$ --- nearly constant at ${\approx}3$ and orders of
magnitude below $d$. A Levina--Bickel MLE
estimator~\cite{levina2004mle} ($k{=}5$) agrees on the relative ordering.
Mean cosine loop closure within video is
$\mathrm{LC}_\mathrm{mean} \approx 0.75$.

\paragraph{Finding 2: a single load-bearing ingredient.}
Across the $21$-cell sweep, the on-sphere uniformity weight
$\lambda_\mathrm{u}$ is the \emph{only} hyperparameter that monotonically
controls geometry quality (\Cref{tab:geometry}). Removing it
($\lambda_\mathrm{u}{=}0$) inflates intrinsic dimension to ${\approx}6$,
drops loop closure to $0.55$, and collapses a downstream linear LVEF probe
$R^2$ from $0.48$ to $0.14$. A no-residual transition init produces the
same collapse signature. Latent dimension, forecast loss, optimization
schedule, and random seed all leave geometry untouched in comparison.
This is a mechanistic claim about which architectural choice in the
training pipeline produces the cardiac-cycle structure visible in
\sonostate{}'s probe.

\paragraph{Finding 3: round-trip error as a complementary collapse detector.}
We fit a post-hoc inverse $g_\phi$ on a holdout set ($n{=}353$ pairs) and
report $\mathrm{RT}_h = \lVert g_\phi^h \circ f_\theta^h(\mathbf{z}) - \mathbf{z}\rVert_1$.
The default cell gives $\mathrm{RT}_{1,2,4,8} \!=\! 0.038, 0.043, 0.049, 0.061$
(sub-linear growth, consistent with a near-isometric flow on the cycle),
while the two collapsing ablations drive $\mathrm{RT}_1$ to ${\sim}10^{-4}$
--- a counter-intuitive ``best'' that, read alongside
$\widehat{d}_\mathrm{ID}$, becomes diagnostic: very low $\mathrm{RT}_1$
paired with very high $\widehat{d}_\mathrm{ID}$ flags representation
collapse, not quality. The two metrics must be read together.

\paragraph{Finding 4: a probe-driven negative on linear phase decoding.}
A closed loop in PCA is suggestive but does not imply linear separability
of cardiac phase across patients. Using the EchoNet-Dynamic ED/ES
keypoints as an unambiguous coarse phase ground truth, a logistic probe on
$\mathbf{z}$ from a single clip recovers ED-vs-ES at chance ($0.42$ vs.\
shuffled $0.48$) for the frozen encoder across all dimensions
($d\!\in\!\{64,128,512\}$); only when the encoder is fine-tuned on the
\sonostate objective does decoding rise to $0.66$. We read this as the
probe surfacing a real limitation of the frozen V-JEPA-2 representation:
phase is encoded as a continuous latent (closed loops) but is not aligned
across patients --- a finding that has direct implications for
phase-conditioned downstream tasks.

% ===== 4. Discussion ====================================================
\section{Discussion}

\paragraph{Implications for safe deployment of medical FMs.}
The three-metric probe ($\widehat{d}_\mathrm{ID}$,
$\mathrm{LC}_\mathrm{mean}$, $\mathrm{RT}_h$) is cheap, label-free, and
decisive: it cleanly separates the default cell (intrinsically $3$-D,
loop-closing, sub-linear round-trip) from the two collapse ablations
that look fine on a forecasting metric but fail catastrophically on
downstream LVEF. A practitioner who shipped the $\lambda_\mathrm{u}{=}0$
checkpoint based on output-level metrics would be deploying a
representation whose internal structure has degenerated. Geometric
probes catch this; standard interpretability does not.

\paragraph{What identity initialization buys us.}
A random-init transition reaches a similar forecast error at convergence,
but it is then ambiguous whether $f_\theta$ is exposing structure of
$E_\phi$ or compensating for noise it added. Identity init eliminates
the ambiguity: the probe is the persistence baseline at $t{=}0$ and any
deviation is information \emph{the encoder} provides. We argue identity
initialization should be the default for any \emph{probe} of latent
dynamics.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
(i) The probe is correlational: closed loops are evidence of a periodic
latent variable, but tying it to ECG-defined phase requires paired data
we do not yet have. (ii) Single backbone (V-JEPA-2 ViT-L/16) and single
dataset (EchoNet-Dynamic); whether the same load-bearing structure
appears in CNN- or contrastive-language-aligned echo backbones is open.
(iii) Forecast evaluation at clip stride is structurally weak (adjacent
clip latents are nearly identical); beat-to-beat retraining is the
obvious next experiment.

\paragraph{Towards intervention-conditioned medical world models.}
A faithful, low-dimensional state space is a precondition for, not a
substitute for, a true intervention-conditioned world model. The $3$-D
cardiac-cycle manifold makes this extension tractable: counterfactual
rollouts under interventions $\mathbf{a}_t$ (a pharmacological step, a
paced beat) can be visualized in PCA, deviations from the unperturbed
loop quantified geometrically, and a small state head is the natural
conditioning point.

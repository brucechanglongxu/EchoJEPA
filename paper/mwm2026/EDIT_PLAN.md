## Headline claim

SonoState is a JEPA-based **latent dynamical world model** for
echocardiography. It (a) recovers the cardiac cycle as a closed loop in
latent space without any ECG supervision, (b) decodes phase (ED vs ES) and
heart rate from the latent angular speed, (c) admits a near-isometric
inverse via cycle consistency, and (d) responds to physics-motivated
acoustic perturbations in geometrically interpretable ways — a stepping
stone to intervention-conditioning.

## Differentiation from the MI4MedFM submission

- MWM emphasis: **forward prediction** as a model of physiology, **path to
  intervention conditioning**, **cycle consistency** as a desirable
  property of a planning-grade dynamics model.
- MI4MedFM emphasis: **post-hoc probe**, **frozen encoder**,
  **interpretability** of an existing FM, **OOD safety monitor**.
- The two papers share the architecture and the cardiac-cycle finding but
  ask different questions.
